# Fizz's quick guide

## Try the preview
Open `/admin`. The banner explains that changes are only a demonstration. Edit a dessert, change its ratings or select Back soon. These changes do not affect your public menu.

## Once your live account is connected
1. Open `/admin` and sign in with the account supplied by your developer.
2. Choose This week, then Edit details beside a dessert.
3. Confirm price, serving size, ingredients, allergens, photo and description. Set all three playful taste ratings.
4. Save, then change availability. If anything important is missing, the page tells you exactly what to finish.
5. Check the public dessert page before sharing it.

Kheer is currently featured with orders closed. Payment and messaging activation require the launch work listed in DECISIONS.md. Do not announce that online orders are open until those checks pass.

## Customer details
With the live database connected, Customers shows your list and Export CSV downloads it with consent records. Exports are logged. Keep this file private. A dessert alert request is not permission for general promotional messages.

## Visual assets
The current packages are illustrations. Supply approved food photos, ingredient lists and allergen details before launch. The site never infers ingredients from those illustrations.
